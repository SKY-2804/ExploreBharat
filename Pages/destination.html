TRAVEL TIPS

Know Before You Go

Essential information to make your journey smooth and memorable.

Carry Warm Clothes

Weather changes quickly in the mountains, even during summer.

Carry Cash

Small cafés and local shops may not accept digital payments.

Drive Carefully

Mountain roads are narrow with sharp turns. Drive slowly.

Check Weather

Always check the weather before visiting Rohtang Pass.
